## 2.1.16 Fewer stuck chats

A reliability update on top of 2.1.15: chats get stuck less often, and when they do, they recover on their own.

### Fixed

- **Messages no longer get stuck in the queue forever.** If ChatGPT never confirms that a message arrived, CoS stops waiting after 15 minutes and lets your next message through. It never sends the same message twice.
- **Automatic continue works right after a page reload**, instead of waiting until the page has fully caught up.
- **Workers near their context limit are no longer given up too early.** A worker that goes quiet while finishing its last task can still complete it.
- **Compact & Resume works for large Project chats.** A big project conversation now gets enough time to load before CoS opens the new chat.
- **Compact & Resume is more robust.** A chat that is still busy is asked to try again in place rather than reloaded, and a handoff that can never be sent gives up after a few attempts instead of reloading the page forever.

### New

- **"New response" marker** in the sidebar for chats that finished while you were looking at something else.
- **Edit the Compact & Resume instructions** in Settings. The default stays exactly as before.
- **Command allowlist or denylist (optional):** decide which programs Core may start. Off by default.
- **Wait for sub-agents (optional):** Goal and Loop can wait until a chat's own workers are done before taking the next step. Off by default.

### Improved

- More of the app is translated into Spanish, French, Japanese, Turkish and Chinese.

**After updating:** reload the Chat On Steroids extension in `chrome://extensions`, then refresh your open ChatGPT tabs.
