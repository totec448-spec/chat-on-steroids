# Plugin catalog artwork

The catalog ships original, code-drawn SVG illustrations in `src/renderer/plugin-icons/`:
`blender.svg`, `memory.svg`, `playwright.svg`, `fetch.svg`, `heygen.svg`, `recraft.svg`,
`unity.svg`, and `custom.svg`. The earlier `time.svg`, `thinking.svg`, `git.svg` and
`filesystem.svg` remain for existing installations whose recipes left Explore.

assets were copied. The icons illustrate the integration's subject (3D, connected
memory, browser automation, web retrieval, and connectivity), and are not official
product marks. Product names identify compatible independent integrations and do not
imply endorsement. This attribution also appears in Settings > Plugins > Legal Notices.

Installed server source, version and license are tracked by the plugin manager. Their
own license files remain in their installation. Reviewed local-package license snapshots
and remote-service terms references are mapped in `docs/licenses/plugins/inventory.json`
and included in the application's Legal Notices inventory. Hosted HeyGen and Recraft
connections are governed by provider terms, not an implied MIT license to their services.
Bundled library licenses and NOTICE files remain part of that same inventory, separately
identified from optional integrations.
